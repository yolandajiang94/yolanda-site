In July 2026, I left the industrial equipment company where I had worked for five years.

At 32, I was preparing for another career transition. This time, I was not looking to leave industrial B2B sales. Instead, I wanted to continue in this field while finding a more sustainable way of working.

### **Continuing in Industrial B2B Sales**

By this point, I had worked with different technical products, from industrial lighting to battery testing equipment.

I had become comfortable with long B2B sales cycles that involved understanding customer needs, learning technical products, communicating across teams, following projects through different stages, and building customer relationships over time.

So in 2026, I was no longer questioning whether I wanted to stay in industrial B2B sales. I wanted to continue building experience in this direction.

### **Looking for Broader Industrial Experience**

After five years in the same industrial equipment field, I had built solid experience in overseas B2B sales, but much of that experience was still concentrated in a relatively specialized product area and customer group.

By 2026, I wanted to broaden that experience. I wanted to move closer to manufacturing, work with a wider range of industries and customers, and continue developing my ability to build business in overseas markets.

I was not looking to start over. I wanted to take the B2B sales experience I had already built into a new environment, where I could learn about different products and continue developing as an industrial B2B salesperson.

That was what I was looking for as I prepared for my next career move in 2026.
